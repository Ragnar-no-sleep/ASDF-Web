Understanding HolDex

How the token tracker works

What is K-Score?

A conviction metric that scores tokens 0-100 based on holder distribution, trading velocity, whale concentration, and on-chain activity. Higher scores indicate stronger holder bases.

What are Metal Ranks?

8 tiers from Diamond (90+) to Rust (<20) that categorize token holder quality at a glance. Ranks use EMA smoothing to prevent manipulation from short-term trading spikes.

What are Credit Ratings?

Grades from A1 (highest conviction) to D (lowest) that summarize a token’s overall investment quality. Based on K-Score, liquidity depth, and holder retention metrics.

How are fees used?

HolDex usage fees are routed to the ASDF burn address, permanently reducing token supply. Every search, every filter, every interaction fuels deflation.
