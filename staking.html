Vesting Glossary

Cliff
Initial lockup period before any tokens unlock. No withdrawals until the cliff ends.
Linear Vesting
Tokens unlock gradually over time at a constant rate after the cliff period.
TGE Unlock
Token Generation Event — percentage released immediately at launch before vesting begins.
Streamflow
On-chain vesting protocol on Solana. All ASDF locks are verifiable via Streamflow’s smart contracts. No custodial risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is token locking?

Tokens are deposited into a Streamflow smart contract with a defined vesting schedule. They cannot be withdrawn or sold until the schedule allows.

Why lock tokens?

Locking signals long-term commitment. It protects holders from sudden sell pressure and earns increased governance voting power.

Can I cancel a lock?

Only if the lock was created with cancellation enabled. Most ASDF locks are non-cancellable to maximize holder confidence.

How does vote weighting work?

Governance power = locked tokens × time multiplier. A 2-year lock earns 4x the voting power of unlocked tokens, incentivizing long-term alignment.
