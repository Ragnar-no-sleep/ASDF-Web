How Burns Work

The deflationary mechanics explained

What is a token burn?

Sending tokens to a dead wallet address that nobody controls. The tokens become permanently inaccessible, reducing total supply forever.

How is it verified?

Every burn is a standard Solana transaction. You can verify any burn on Solscan by checking the destination address. Don’t trust — verify.

Why burn tokens?

Fewer tokens in circulation means each remaining token represents a larger share of the total supply. Burns are the simplest deflationary mechanism.

Can burns be reversed?

No. The burn address has no private key. Once tokens arrive there, they are gone permanently. This is by design — immutability is the point.
