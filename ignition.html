How are airdrops distributed?

Weekly airdrops are distributed proportionally based on your registered token’s 24h volume and multiplier tier. Higher volume = bigger share.

What is KOTH?

King of the Pill — the AI-selected champion token receiving 10% of the airdrop pool. Selection is based on volume, holder growth, and community activity.

What is Robinhood Partnership?

External token creators can share their Pump.fun creator fees with the ASDF airdrop pool. In return, their token holders qualify for airdrops.

Can I create multiple tokens?

Yes. Each token must be registered separately. Launch fee is 0.02 SOL per token. Each registered token earns its own multiplier based on volume.
